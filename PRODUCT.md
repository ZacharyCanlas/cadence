# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users
Recreational runners, the author and friends, who want to raise their cadence a few steps at a time. They set the app up standing still, then run with the phone locked in a pocket and their own music playing.

## Product Purpose
A running metronome that keeps exact time over the runner's music for a whole run, with the screen off, without pausing or ducking the music. Success: the beat and Spotify play together for an hour, and the runner's cadence rises across weeks.

## Positioning
It never takes audio focus, so music players keep playing at full volume and keep the earbud buttons. The cadence ramp climbs gradually across a run instead of jumping to a target, and the cadence you chose is never overwritten by the ramp.

## Operating Context
Before the run: choose cadence, sound, accent and ramp. During: phone locked, music app in front, glances at the notification; ±1 and Stop from the lock screen. Calls silence the beat; disconnecting earbuds stops it.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Cadence 100–220, tap tempo, six presets, eight synthesised sounds, accents every 2/3/4/6, vibration, cadence ramp, session clock and step count. Sideloaded APK, minSdk 33, English only. No account, no network, no tracking.

## Evidence on Hand
Coaching research behind each feature in docs/cadence-training.md. No testimonials, user counts or benchmarks exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
- The runner's music comes first: never interrupt it.
- The beat is exact or it is nothing.
- A setting you chose is never silently changed.
- Every control traces back to something runners actually do.
